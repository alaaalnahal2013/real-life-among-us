# Tasks

1. **Arranging the cups:** Take 3 cups from the kitchen and arrange them on the table.
2. **Filling the water:** Fill a glass with water and place it in a specific location.
3. **Recycling:** Throw 3 empty cans in the trash can.
4. **Targeting:** Throw a ball (or a small object) into the laundry basket from a distance of 3 steps.
5. **Quick puzzle:** Assemble 4 puzzle pieces or rearrange scattered papers on the table.
6. **Balance:** Walk along the carpet/rug line while carrying a book on your head without it falling.
7. **Solve the puzzle:** Write the solution to a very simple math problem (for example: $12 \times 4$) on a piece of paper placed on the wall.
8. **Number order:** Arrange 5 numbered slips of paper on the table from smallest to largest.
9. **Search for the object:** Look for a specific key or small object hidden in the hall and place it where the task is.
10. **Quick drawing:** Draw a simple shape (such as a house, car, or tree) on the board/paper.
11. **Book arrangement:** Rearrange 3 books by size on the shelf.
12. **Tie the knot:** Tie a small rope or shawl in a certain way and untie it.
