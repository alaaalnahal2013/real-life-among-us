# Rules

- **Imposter Kills:** The way the imposter kills the crewmates is by a brief touch on the shoulder. Between each kill, there is a 40-second cooldown.
- **Silence on Death:** You can't scream or say anything once the imposter kills you.
- **Round Silence:** You can't speak during the round.
- **If Eliminated:** If you got killed, sit down and don't make a sound.

*Good Luck!*
